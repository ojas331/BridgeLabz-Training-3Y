Welcome to Mathura

Mathura is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh known for its rich culture, temples and connection with Lord Krishna.

History of Mathura

Mathura is one of the ancient cities of India. It has played an important role in Indian history, religion and art.

The city is especially famous for its association with Lord Krishna and the Krishna Janmabhoomi.

Famous Places

Krishna Janmabhoomi

A major religious site believed to be the birthplace of Lord Krishna.

Dwarkadhish Temple

A beautiful temple known for its architecture and religious significance.

Vishram Ghat

A famous ghat located on the banks of the Yamuna River.

Culture and Festivals

Mathura is famous for Holi, Janmashtami, traditional music, dance and colourful religious celebrations.
